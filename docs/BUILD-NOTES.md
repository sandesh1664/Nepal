# Nepal — Beyond the Ordinary

A cinematic, responsive Nepal travel website built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. The deployed directory is `dist/`. No build step or package installation is required. Three.js r170 is vendored locally with its MIT license.

## Features

- High-resolution, locally hosted photographs of Nepal, with photographer credits.
- Three locally hosted aerial films with a motion-toggle hero and dedicated cinema viewer.
- Real spherical 360° photographs of Gosaikunda and Ram Mandir with drag, keyboard, zoom, rotation, reset and expanded-view controls.
- An explicitly artistic procedural 3D Himalayan flight, rendered with Three.js or a Canvas 2D software projection when WebGL is unavailable.
- A 109-chapter photo journal with playback, keyboard navigation and reduced-motion support.
- Seven province guides, four trekking introductions, and a community-focused culture feature.
- An original AI-created Himalayan dreamscape, clearly labeled in the site.
- One hundred and nine destination guides with category filters and saved places, including eleven Far West guides, sixteen Koshi guides, twelve Madhesh guides, sixteen Bagmati guides, eighteen Gandaki guides, eighteen Lumbini guides and eighteen Karnali guides. Original national place identities are retained when a regional chapter expands their coverage.
- Four seasonal guides with accessible tabs.
- A touring itinerary builder with 7, 10 and 14-day options, four interests and seasonal notes.
- Browser-local saved itineraries and plain-text downloads.
- Responsive navigation, native modal dialogs and practical travel FAQs.

This is an independent travel inspiration site. It has no booking, payment, lead collection or server database. Browser storage is specific to the visitor's browser and origin. Itineraries are editable inspiration, not verified travel arrangements or high-altitude trekking schedules.

## Immersive media and performance

All videos, posters, panoramas and generated artwork are locally hosted. Videos are silent H.264 MP4s; they are not 360° videos. The panorama experiences use genuine equirectangular photographs captured in 2019. The 3D flight is a procedural imagined landscape, not a geographic reconstruction. The AI dreamscape is also an artistic interpretation rather than a photograph of a specific place.

Exact video/panorama URLs, creators, licenses and transformations are recorded in `dist/assets/immersive/media-credits.json` and visible in the site's credits dialog. Gosaikunda and Ram Mandir panoramas retain CC BY-SA 4.0. Films use the Pexels License. Three.js uses the MIT license in `dist/vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt`.

The interactive renderer loads only when requested. Videos pause outside the visible hero, when a dialog opens, and when the page is hidden. Reduced-motion and data-saving preferences suppress automatic hero motion. The viewer pauses off screen; the software renderer limits its refresh rate and does not redraw a paused, unchanged camera.
